## Blocker

1. **Cross-origin navigation waits 10 s and falsely warns.**
   - Outside the desktop app, a widget's windowId lives in sessionStorage, which is per-origin. A tab
     that moves to another origin (even another localhost port) reconnects under a NEW windowId.
   - The wait watched only the old id, so it timed out after 10 s (measured 10,095 ms, reconnect after
     ~20 ms) and returned "no widget reconnected". `--strict` then fails a navigation that worked.
   - Hits `--headless`'s first navigation, dev-server port changes, redirect flows, and iframe windows.
   - The test runner's `navigate` step had the same flaw before this release.

## Major

- **The new warning overwrites existing warnings and keeps a stale `warningRepeated: true`** (blast
  radius, correctness; reproduced). A hidden-tab or ambiguous-focus warning is lost, and `hj` can
  print nothing at all.
- **A desktop cold start reports `reconnected: true` for the unchanged page** (blast radius;
  reproduced). `commandTarget` ran before the need-window wait, and a null target accepted any
  connected browser.
- **A fixed ~400 ms floor on every navigate/refresh** (efficiency; measured 407 ms against a 21 ms
  real reconnect). The 100 ms + 300 ms sleeps are dead time: the widget accepts commands as soon as
  it has sent `connected`.

## Minor

- Security (LOW): a caller-supplied `timeout` has no cap, and the wait loop outlives a disconnected
  client. Measured: 5,350 aborted requests held about 100 MB.
  - Same class as `/wait`, `/click` autoWait and `/request`; any page can reach it (#44).
- The test runner's `navigate` step still picks its window by its own rule. `pickTargetWindow`'s "ONE
  definition" claim is false while that twin exists.
- `data.reconnected`, `data.sameDocument`, `--no-wait` and `--timeout` are not in SKILL.md or
  `hj --help`.
- The CHANGELOG files a 10 s-per-navigate cost for pages without the widget under "Fixed". It should
  say it plainly under "Changed".
- A hash-only navigation in the desktop app (Electron branch) never computes `sameDocument`. An empty
  fragment (`/h#`) is not detected either.
- The build prints no size delta (carried over, #1076). The fixture's page server binds all
  interfaces.
- Pre-existing: after a navigate, focus can move to another tab or an iframe.
